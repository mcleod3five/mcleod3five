03 / Questions

Good to know.

Do I always need planning permission for an extension?

Not always. Some extensions may qualify as permitted development, depending on the property, its history and the proposed works. We'll help identify what needs checking before you commit to a design.

Can you help with an existing home that needs reworking?

Yes. A renovation can be about improving circulation, daylight and how rooms connect, not just adding floor area.
