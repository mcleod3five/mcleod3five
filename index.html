About

Led by Georgina McLeod.

mcleod3five is led by Georgina McLeod, a clean, practical architect with many years' experience across the residential and commercial sectors.

The studio brings together architectural design, project coordination and digital design tools to turn early ideas into clear, achievable proposals. The approach is calm, collaborative and practical: good ideas, well drawn, with fewer surprises along the way.
