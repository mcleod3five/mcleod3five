About

Led by Georgina McLeod, architect and digital innovator.

mcleod3five brings together architectural design, project coordination and digital innovation. The studio works with homeowners, schools, colleges, local organisations and community clients to turn early ideas into clear, achievable design proposals.

With experience across residential, education, community and funded projects, the approach is calm, collaborative and practical: good ideas, well drawn, with fewer surprises along the way.

Process

Simple, structured and human.

  1. ListenUnderstand the brief, budget, site and opportunities.
  2. DesignDevelop clear options and planning-ready drawings.
  3. CoordinateWork with consultants, Building Control and contractors.
  4. DeliverSupport the project through tender, construction and completion.
