About

Clean, considered architecture with practical delivery built in.

Led by Georgina McLeod, mcleod3five is an architecture and project management studio based in Hove, East Sussex.

With many years of experience across the residential, commercial and community sectors, the studio helps clients turn early ideas into clear, achievable design proposals and well-managed projects.

The approach is calm, collaborative and practical: good ideas, well drawn, with fewer surprises along the way.
