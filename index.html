About the Studio

Projects with purpose, polish and a plan.

mcleod3five is an architecture and project management studio based at MODA Hove, working across Brighton & Hove and the South East.

Led by Georgina McLeod, the studio brings many years of experience across residential, commercial and community sectors, helping clients turn early ideas into clear, achievable design proposals and well-managed projects.
