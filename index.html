01 / About

Good design starts with a conversation.

McLeod35 Architecture is led by Georgina, a chartered architect with two decades of experience across residential, commercial and education projects, from first ideas through to completion.

With a specialist degree in advanced environmental studies, Georgina brings a considered approach to every brief. She works with a trusted team of consultants and technicians built up over many years, offering a friendly, dependable service tailored to your project.
