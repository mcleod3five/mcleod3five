Technical drawings and specifications may be needed alongside structural engineering and other specialist designs. We can establish a coordinated scope appropriate to the project.

03 / Questions

Good to know.

Is planning permission the same as building regulations approval?

No. Planning and building regulations are separate systems with different purposes. A project may need one, both or neither, depending on the work.

Do your fees include structural calculations?

Specialist structural engineering is normally a separate service. We can help identify the information needed and agree the scope before work begins.
