01 / Our approach

Practical advice. Thoughtful design.

Understand the brief

We begin with how the space needs to function: its users, activities, budget, programme and existing constraints. That gives the design a clear purpose.

Design with the bigger picture in mind

Commercial projects can involve access, servicing, fire safety, planning and coordination with specialist consultants. We identify the key questions early and agree the appropriate project team.

A flexible architectural service

Our experience spans commercial, community and education settings. We can discuss feasibility studies, concept proposals, planning drawings and later-stage design support as separate services.
