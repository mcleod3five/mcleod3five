01 / Our approach

Practical advice. Thoughtful design.

Understand the planning context

We review the proposal in relation to the property and its surroundings, including the relevant local planning considerations. Some projects benefit from an early feasibility review or pre-application discussion.

Drawings that communicate the design

Depending on the project, planning information may include existing and proposed plans, elevations, sections and other supporting material. We agree the required package before starting.

Support through the application

We can prepare and coordinate the architectural elements of a planning submission and respond to design queries during the process, within the agreed appointment. The local planning authority makes the decision.

03 / Questions

Good to know.

Does a planning application guarantee permission?

No. Planning decisions are made by the local planning authority. Good preparation can help explain a proposal clearly but cannot guarantee an approval.

Can you help with a commercial planning proposal?

Yes. We work on residential and commercial architecture and can discuss the planning requirements for the specific project.
